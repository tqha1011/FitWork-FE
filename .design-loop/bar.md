# Design bar

1. Give the hero headline at least three times the supporting text's size, with a short line length.
2. Keep one filled cobalt action in each decision group.
3. Place a legible product preview beside the desktop hero; stack it below the copy on mobile.
4. Separate major marketing sections with generous whitespace and clear heading changes.
5. Use neutral surfaces, fine borders, and restrained rounding to separate interface regions.
6. Collapse navigation on mobile while keeping the primary action and content readable.
